About Us

Locally owned, family run, and proud to serve the community we live in.

CL Professional Pet Services LLC was founded right here in St. Peters with one simple idea: your time with your dog should be spent playing, not scooping.

We're not a national franchise — when you call (636) 375-4342, you talk to the people who actually clean your yard. We show up on schedule, do the job thoroughly, and treat your property (and your pets) like our own.

We're fully licensed and insured, and we're proud to offer ongoing discounts to military members, veterans, and first responders.
