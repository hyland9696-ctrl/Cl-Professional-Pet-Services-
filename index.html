Terms of Service

CL Professional Pet Services LLC · St. Peters, Missouri

1. Services. CL Professional Pet Services LLC ("CLPPS") provides pet waste removal at the service address you provide, on the schedule you select (twice a week, weekly, every other week, or one-time). Every visit includes a full-yard sweep, waste haul-away, and securing your gate before we leave.

2. Quotes & pricing. Quotes are honored for 6 months from the date issued and assume a serviceable yard of up to ¼ acre. Lot size may be verified against county records; if your yard is materially larger, we will contact you to agree on adjusted pricing before service begins. New recurring clients are normally charged a one-time $40 initial clean on the first invoice; this fee is currently waived for new recurring customers. A yard with excessive accumulation, or a first cleanup needing significantly more time than a standard visit, may incur an additional cleanup charge, which we will quote and agree with you before any extra work is done. There are no hidden fees.

3. Billing. Recurring service is billed monthly through our billing system using the payment method you place on file via a secure link. A late fee of $10 per month applies to invoices remaining unpaid 30 days after the due date.

4. No contracts. Service is month-to-month. You may cancel at any time with no cancellation fee.

5. Yard access. Please ensure gates are unlocked and pets are secured on your service day. If we cannot access the yard, the visit may be skipped and charged; we will notify you and attempt to reschedule where possible.

6. Weather & safety. Service may be delayed for severe weather or unsafe conditions; missed visits are rescheduled or credited.

7. Liability. CLPPS is licensed and insured. Our liability for any claim arising from services is limited to the amount paid for the month in which the claim arose.

8. Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Missouri.
