Accessibility

Last reviewed 11 September 2026

We are a small pet waste removal business in St. Peters, Missouri. We want anybody who wants our help to be able to ask for it — whether they use a screen reader, navigate with a keyboard, need larger text, or simply find websites hard going.

If any part of this site gets in your way, don’t fight with it. Call or text us and we will do the whole thing with you over the phone — the quote, the price, the sign-up, all of it. You will get exactly the same price you would have got online. There is no catch and no extra charge.

What we aim for

We work to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. That is the standard the U.S. Department of Justice points to for websites, and it is what we test against.

What we have done

  • Every page is checked against WCAG 2.1 AA automatically, and the check runs again every time we change the site, so problems are caught before they go live.
  • Text and background colours are tested to make sure there is enough contrast to read comfortably.
  • The whole site, including the quote form, can be used with a keyboard alone, and you can always see which control you are on.
  • Pages work at up to 400% zoom without you having to scroll sideways to read a line.
  • Images carry text descriptions, and form fields carry labels that a screen reader reads out.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, we honour that setting.

Where we know we fall short

We would rather tell you this plainly than claim to be perfect:

  • Automated testing catches roughly a third of accessibility problems. The rest needs a person, and we have not yet had this site tested by somebody who uses a screen reader every day. We intend to.
  • Our address look-up on the quote form relies on a mapping service we do not control. If it is awkward to use, type your address in by hand — it works the same — or call us and we will take it down for you.
  • Some of what we link to (our Facebook page, for example) belongs to other companies, and we cannot control how accessible those are.

Telling us about a problem

If something on this site did not work for you, we want to know, and we are not going to be defensive about it. Tell us what page you were on and what happened, in whatever detail suits you.
