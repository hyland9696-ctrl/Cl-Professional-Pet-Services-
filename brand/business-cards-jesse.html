Jesse Duncan — 20 cards, double sided

Page 1 is the details side, page 2 is the logo side. 3.5″ × 2″, ten per page.

  1. Print double sided, and choose flip on long edge.
  2. Set scale to 100% — not “fit to page”, or the cards come out the wrong size.
  3. Use white cardstock, or Avery 8371 / 5371 perforated stock, which this layout matches.
  4. Turn on background graphics in the print dialog so the green side prints.
