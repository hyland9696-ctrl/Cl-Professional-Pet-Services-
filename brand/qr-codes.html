Yard sign QR codes

One code per city, so the lead tracker can tell you how many people scanned in each town.

For the sign shop. Each PNG is 1184 × 1184 at 600 dpi — that prints at just under 2 inches square, the same size as the code on the current signs. Print it at least 1.5 inches square and keep the white border around it; that quiet zone is part of the code. Do not stretch it, recolour it, or lay anything over it. Everything else on the sign stays as it is.
Print the three-letter tag under each code — the STP / OFA / WNZ badge shown here. Once the codes differ by city the signs stop being interchangeable, and a batch that ends up in the wrong town quietly ruins the numbers. The tag lets anyone tell the piles apart at a glance.
The signs already in the ground keep working. Their code still reports as a sign scan — it just shows up without a city beside it. Nothing needs pulling or replacing.
