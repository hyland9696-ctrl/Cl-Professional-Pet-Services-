Why the sign link is three letters. /stp rather than /stpeters keeps the printed code at 29 squares instead of 33, so each square is bigger and scans more easily from a few feet away in a front yard. It forwards straight to the full link underneath, and the campaign name in your tracker is still the full word.
Both links do the same job. The short one goes on the sign. Use the full one anywhere you need a normal clickable link — a Facebook post, an email, a Google Business post — and it will be counted under the same city.
