How a price is worked out

1. Start with the weekly base rate for the number of dogs.
1 dog $85 · 2 dogs $91 · 3 dogs $97 · 4 dogs $103 · 5 or more is a custom quote.
2. Multiply by the customer’s ZIP tier. Value ×0.90 · Core ×0.95 · Standard ×1.00 · Premium ×1.10. A ZIP that is not on the list is priced as Value.
3. Adjust for the plan.
Weekly = that figure.   Every other week = ×0.82.   Twice a week = the weekly price ×1.80 (double, less the 10% discount).
Monthly is its own base — 1 dog $65, 2 $70, 3 $75, 4 $80 — then the same ZIP tier.
4. Round to the nearest whole dollar. Every quoted price is a whole number.
5. One-time cleanups ignore the ZIP tier and are priced by time on site instead.

Worked examples

  1. 2 dogs, weekly, ZIP 63304 (Premium)
    $91 × 1.10 = $100.10 → $100
    First invoice $140 with the $40 initial clean.
  2. 3 dogs, every other week, ZIP 63031 (Value)
    $97 × 0.90 × 0.82 = $71.59 → $72
    First invoice $112 with the $40 initial clean.
  3. 1 dog, twice a week, ZIP 63376 (Standard)
    $85 × 1.00 = $85 weekly, ×1.80 = $153.00 → $153
    First invoice $193 with the $40 initial clean.
