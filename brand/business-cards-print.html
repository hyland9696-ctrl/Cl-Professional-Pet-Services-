Business cards — files for the printer

PNG, 600 dpi, four files per person — a front and a back in two versions.

Ask them one question first

“Do you want the artwork with bleed or at finished size?” Send the with bleed files unless they say finished size. Send the wrong one and the card gets trimmed in the wrong place — either a white sliver down the edge of the green side, or your details cropped closer than intended.

What to tell your supplier

Finished size3.5″ × 2″ (standard US business card)
“With bleed” files2250 × 1350 px — 3.75″ × 2.25″ at 600 dpi, 0.125″ bleed all round
“Finished size” files2100 × 1200 px — 3.5″ × 2″ at 600 dpi, no bleed
Resolution600 dpi — double the 300 dpi minimum, so they can downsample
SidesDouble sided. One file is the details, the other the green logo side
Safe areaAll text sits at least 0.125″ inside the trim
ColourRGB — brand green #0E4B3B, accent #1D8A68. Ask them to convert to CMYK
Suggested stock16pt matte or soft-touch. The green back looks best matte or uncoated
