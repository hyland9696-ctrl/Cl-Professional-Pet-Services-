Business cards — files for the printer

Send the printer the PDF. It is already the right size with bleed, and the font is embedded, so nothing needs installing at their end.

What to tell your supplier

Finished size3.5″ × 2″ (standard US business card)
File size supplied3.75″ × 2.25″ — includes 0.125″ bleed on all four sides
Safe areaAll text sits at least 0.125″ inside the trim
SidesDouble sided. Page 1 is the details, page 2 is the green logo side
ResolutionVector PDF with the font embedded. PNGs, if they want them, are 600 dpi
ColourRGB — brand green is #0E4B3B, accent green #1D8A68. Ask them to convert to CMYK
Suggested stock16pt matte or soft-touch. The green back looks best uncoated or matte
One thing worth asking them: the files are RGB, which nearly every printer accepts and converts. If they want CMYK specifically, send them the PDF anyway and ask them to convert — the deep green may shift very slightly on press, which is normal. Ask for a proof before the full run.
